# AI assistance

Parts of this software, its tests and its documentation were drafted with the assistance
of Claude large language models (Anthropic), under the direction of the authors.

- **Design, audit and planning (November 2026):** an independent audit of the earlier
  `coare35vn_ice` code against the source literature, the software design, the
  verification study design and the task backlog.

The authors review the physics, the parameter values and all results, and take full
responsibility for the content. The models are credited here, in the `CITATION.cff`
contributors note and in `Co-Authored-By` commit trailers. They are not authors of any
associated paper.
